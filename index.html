Smarter Campus. Greener Future.

Built for DSU Code Clash 2.0 (Problem Statement: AI-Powered Sustainable Campus). EcoCampus AI is an enterprise-grade sustainability command center uniting visual incident triage, a real-time 2D digital twin, grounded operational intelligence, and defensible resource calculations.

Ethical Notice to Judges & Participants: In accordance with DSU Code Clash 2.0 guidelines, the participant has verified that AI-assisted design and scaffolding tools are permitted under hackathon regulations. All algorithmic logic, heuristic rules, formulas, UI components, and mock architectures have been engineered transparently. No proprietary or uncredited third-party code has been incorporated.

📷

1. Computer Vision Triage

Provides photo-based visible-clue triage. Operates transparently with honest heuristic bounding cues and limitation disclosures. Explains that surface photos indicate anomalies but do not substitute for sub-surface meter telemetry.

🗺️

2. 2D Interactive Digital Twin

An interactive vector schematic mapping 8 campus zones to a single source of truth. Every zone is keyboard accessible and dynamically reflects active incident counts, severity weighting, and health scores (0-100%).

🤖

3. Grounded AI Copilot

A consultative sustainability assistant grounded strictly in current live campus state. Generates facilities morning briefings, analyzes emergency hotspots, and drafts SOPs without hallucinations or external privacy exposure.

🎯 3-Minute Judge Demonstration Script

Follow this step-by-step walkthrough to experience the complete integrated workflow:

Minute 1: Overview & Interactive Digital Twin

Navigate to the Overview Dashboard to view live KPIs, category donut, and weekly trends. Next, click the Digital Twin Map. Notice that Student Amenities & Washrooms is highlighted in RED (Critical) due to an active flush valve leak (`ECO-2026-001`). Click on the zone to inspect the live drawer, health score, and recommended actions.

Minute 2: Visual Triage & Dynamic Reporting

Switch to Computer Vision. Pick the "Cafeteria Overflowing Organics Bin" preset or upload an image. Observe the heuristic bounding box and clue checklist. Click "Transfer Diagnosis to Issue Report Form". Notice the form is pre-filled with category, severity, and photo. Hit Submit Report to watch the new issue instantly register!

Minute 3: Resolution Tracking, AI Copilot & Audit

Open the Campus Action Plan or Issue Ledger. Open issue `ECO-2026-001` and mark its status as Resolved. Return to the Digital Twin or Dashboard: the washroom zone instantly turns GREEN (Good) and health recovers to 100%! Finally, open the Grounded AI Copilot and click "Draft Facilities Morning Briefing" to see an updated executive summary.
